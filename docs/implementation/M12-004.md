# M12-004 — Listing Action Effectiveness & Operational Learning Layer

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Measure how governed M12-001→003 proposals, approvals, executions, verification outcomes, reconciliation cases, and rollback results relate to observed operational outcomes without rewriting history, inferring causation from association, or changing live execution policy automatically.

## Accepted operational learning capabilities

M12-004 now:

- registers immutable operational episodes;
- binds episodes to exact proposal, approval, authority, execution, verification, reconciliation, and rollback fingerprints;
- registers immutable observed operational outcomes;
- measures execution attempts, success, duplicate suppression, and outcome-unknown frequency;
- measures verification volume, mismatch frequency, and verification outcome-unknown frequency;
- measures reconciliation cases and unresolved/open reconciliation;
- measures rollback attempts, success, and rollback outcome-unknown frequency;
- records all outcome relationships as associations, never causal claims;
- keeps unknown and unresolved outcomes explicit;
- emits advisory-only M12-005 improvement candidates.

## Candidate boundary

Every improvement candidate is:

- `advisory_only: true`;
- `promotion_status: NOT_PROMOTED`;
- traceable to governed metrics and exact source fingerprints;
- explicitly non-causal;
- incapable of changing live execution policy.

## Operational boundary

M12-004 does not:

- rewrite M12-001→003 history;
- infer seller intent or approval;
- determine or change listing price or terms;
- approve, authorize, execute, publish, schedule, queue, or transmit an external action;
- promote a calibration candidate automatically.

All learning output remains:

- `public_eligible: false`;
- `external_action_capability: NONE`.
