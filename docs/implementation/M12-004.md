# M12-004 — Listing Action Effectiveness & Operational Learning Layer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Measure how governed M12-001→003 proposals, approvals, executions, verification outcomes, reconciliation cases, and rollback results relate to observed operational outcomes without rewriting historical records, inferring causation from association, or changing live execution policy automatically.

## Frozen boundary

M12-004 may:

- register governed operational outcomes;
- link outcomes to exact proposal, approval, authority, execution, verification, reconciliation, and rollback fingerprints;
- measure execution/verification/reconciliation effectiveness;
- distinguish observation from association and hypothesis;
- quantify duplicate suppression, outcome-unknown frequency, verification mismatch frequency, and rollback behavior;
- surface advisory operational-improvement candidates for M12-005 calibration.

M12-004 does not:

- rewrite M12-001→003 history;
- infer causation from correlation;
- infer seller intent or approval;
- change listing price or terms;
- alter execution policy automatically;
- auto-promote calibration changes;
- execute, publish, schedule, queue, or transmit external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M12-004 may be accepted only when operational outcomes remain immutable and traceable, associations are explicitly non-causal, unknown/missing outcomes remain explicit, historical records are untouched, improvement candidates remain advisory only, no live policy changes occur, and inherited certification gates remain green.
