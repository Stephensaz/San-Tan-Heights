# M8-010 — Diagram Grammar Runtime

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Define the locked schematic vocabulary and validation runtime consumed by later lot/context diagram renderers.

## Grammar boundary

The runtime expresses governed relationships only. It is not a map engine and does not create geographic precision.

Allowed entity vocabulary includes subject lots, adjacent lots, roads, common tracts, open space, washes, community boundaries, and orientation markers.

Allowed relationship vocabulary includes fronts, backs-to, sides-to, touches, adjacent-to, across-from, and oriented-toward.

## Guardrails

- Exactly one `SUBJECT_LOT` is required.
- Every entity and relationship requires a governed source finding reference.
- Relationship references must resolve to known entities.
- Coordinates are prohibited at the grammar layer.
- Distances are prohibited at the grammar layer.
- Value, premium, desirability, and other price-like claims are prohibited.
- Grammar specs and registry fingerprints are deterministic.
- Actual visual placement belongs to M8-011.
