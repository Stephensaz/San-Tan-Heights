# M12-006 — Listing Execution Workspace & Operational Command Center

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Assemble current M12 action plans, approvals, execution states, verification/reconciliation cases, corrective actions, adapter health, blocked work, rollback eligibility, and historical receipts into one listing- and portfolio-level operational workspace.

## Frozen boundary

M12-006 may:

- surface work requiring human decision;
- surface approved but not executed actions;
- surface executing and completed actions;
- surface verification/reconciliation-required work;
- surface stale approvals and expired authority;
- surface outcome-unknown cases;
- surface execution holds and rollback eligibility;
- provide precise next-safe-action guidance;
- preserve full lineage back to Seller Intelligence and M12-001→005 artifacts.

M12-006 does not:

- approve;
- authorize;
- execute;
- publish;
- schedule;
- queue;
- transmit;
- mutate accepted policy;
- bypass human authority;
- infer seller approval;
- determine price or terms.

## Acceptance boundary

M12-006 may be accepted only when workspace state is a pure governed projection of certified upstream artifacts, every item is placed in exactly one operational state, next-safe-action guidance is non-authorizing, stale/expired/unknown/blocked states remain explicit, rollback eligibility is never confused with rollback authorization, and inherited certification gates remain green.
