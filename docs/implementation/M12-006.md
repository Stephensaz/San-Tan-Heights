# M12-006 — Listing Execution Workspace & Operational Command Center

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Assemble current M12 action plans, approvals, execution states, verification/reconciliation cases, corrective work, adapter health, blocked work, rollback eligibility, and historical receipts into one listing- and portfolio-level operational workspace.

## Accepted workspace model

M12-006 now projects each governed action into exactly one operational state:

- `NEEDS_HUMAN_DECISION`
- `APPROVED_NOT_EXECUTED`
- `EXECUTING`
- `VERIFICATION_RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`
- `BLOCKED`
- `COMPLETE`

The workspace remains a visibility and orchestration layer only.

## Explicit operational visibility

The workspace surfaces:

- stale approvals;
- expired or invalid authority;
- adapter health;
- blocked work;
- execution outcome-unknown;
- verification mismatch/unknown;
- open reconciliation cases;
- rollback eligibility;
- immutable historical execution/rollback receipts;
- full lineage back to Seller Intelligence and M12-001→005 artifacts.

Rollback eligibility is explicitly distinct from rollback authorization.

## Next-safe-action boundary

Each state receives deterministic next-safe-action guidance such as:

- review and decide;
- revalidate authority and review execution readiness;
- wait for execution receipt;
- review verification or reconciliation;
- review blocking condition;
- no action required.

These are visibility instructions only. They do not approve, authorize, execute, publish, schedule, queue, transmit, or mutate policy.

## Authority boundary

M12-006 has:

- no approval method;
- no authorization method;
- no execution method;
- no publication/scheduling/queue/transmission method;
- no price or terms fields;
- no seller-approval field;
- `public_eligible: false`;
- `external_action_capability: NONE`.
