# M11-004 — Seller Decision Timeline & Adaptive Monitoring Engine

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Take certified M11-002 strategy and M11-003 scenario context and build a governed seller decision timeline that detects meaningful changes in certified evidence, strategy state, scenario sensitivity, and review checkpoints over time without automatically changing strategy or taking external action.

## Frozen boundary

M11-004 may:

- register dated strategy snapshots;
- compare current versus prior certified seller-strategy state;
- identify newly triggered or cleared Day-7 / Day-10 / Day-14 review conditions;
- identify meaningful changes in buyer alternatives, competitive pressure, buyer depth, differentiation, new-construction pressure, pricing-response environment, and week-over-week direction;
- mark evidence as stale, missing, superseded, or newly current;
- produce a human-review-required monitoring event;
- preserve exact snapshot, strategy, scenario, and evidence lineage.

M11-004 does not:

- recommend or change a list price;
- predict a sale price or guaranteed proceeds;
- infer seller motivation, acceptance, or buyer behavior;
- infer protected-class information;
- convert monitoring changes into autonomous decisions;
- expose seller strategy publicly by default;
- execute, schedule, queue, approve, publish, or authorize external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M11-004 may be accepted only when timeline events are deterministic and chronologically valid, changes are derived only from certified snapshots, stale/missing evidence remains explicit, scenario assumptions stay distinguishable from facts, human-review events are non-prescriptive, prohibited price/value/outcome fields cannot be generated, public eligibility remains false, external action capability remains NONE, and inherited certification gates pass.
