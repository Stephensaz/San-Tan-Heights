# M11-004 — Seller Decision Timeline & Adaptive Monitoring Engine

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Take certified M11-002 strategy and M11-003 scenario context and build a governed seller decision timeline that detects meaningful changes in certified evidence, strategy state, scenario sensitivity, and review checkpoints over time without automatically changing strategy or taking external action.

## Accepted capabilities

M11-004 now:

- registers dated certified seller-strategy snapshots;
- orders them chronologically;
- marks prior snapshots explicitly superseded by a newer certified snapshot;
- compares current versus prior buyer-alternative fingerprints;
- detects competitive-pressure changes;
- detects changes across buyer depth, verified differentiation, new-construction pressure, pricing-response environment, and week-over-week direction;
- identifies newly triggered or cleared Day-7 / Day-10 / Day-14 review conditions;
- identifies evidence becoming newly current, missing, or suppressed/stale;
- emits a governed `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` monitoring event when meaningful changes occur.

Implemented in:

- `registries/seller_intelligence/m11-004-seller-decision-timeline-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/seller_intelligence/timeline.py`
- `tests/unit/seller_intelligence/test_timeline.py`
- `certification-evidence/m11-004/seller-decision-timeline-acceptance-v1.0.json`

## Lineage boundary

Certified strategy/evidence lineage remains separate from hypothetical M11-003 scenario lineage.

Scenario assumptions may travel with a snapshot for context, but they cannot be promoted into certified facts.

## Monitoring boundary

A monitoring event can only require human review.

It cannot:

- recommend or execute a price change;
- alter strategy automatically;
- predict a sale price;
- guarantee proceeds;
- infer seller motivation, acceptance, or buyer behavior;
- publish information;
- authorize an external action.

Every monitoring event remains non-public and has `external_action_capability: NONE`.
