# M9-001 — Production Corpus Manifest & Admission Bootstrap

Status: NOT_STARTED

## Purpose

Freeze the exact governed production property corpus and the admission contract before any production population begins.

## Required outputs

- immutable corpus manifest;
- deterministic corpus-manifest fingerprint;
- exact source snapshot references;
- canonical property identifier for every corpus member;
- admission-state enum;
- quarantine-reason enum;
- exclusion-reason enum;
- full accounting invariant;
- schema validation;
- negative controls for missing/duplicate property identity;
- negative controls for unclassified records;
- lineage binding to approved source snapshots.

## Admission states

- ADMITTED
- QUARANTINED
- EXCLUDED_WITH_GOVERNED_REASON

## Hard rules

- no property may be silently dropped;
- no property may appear twice;
- no address text may substitute for canonical identity;
- no unresolved identity may be admitted;
- no missing lineage may be admitted;
- no publication occurs in M9-001;
- no report generation occurs in M9-001;
- no new intelligence is derived in M9-001.

## Acceptance boundary

M9-001 is accepted only when the corpus manifest is deterministic, fully accounted, schema-valid, lineage-bound, and fail-closed under intentionally corrupted membership and admission fixtures.
