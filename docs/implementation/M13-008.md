# M13-008 — Historical Replay, Calibration, Drift & Failure Controls

Status: IMPLEMENTED — AWAITING ACCEPTANCE

## Purpose

M13-008 tests the certified Milestone 13 intelligence system against historical states and degraded-input conditions. It measures whether outputs remain reproducible and whether failures, corrections, conflicts, stale evidence, missing evidence, delays, and duplicates remain explicit rather than being silently normalized.

## Scope

The governed replay covers calm and rapid-change market regimes plus normal, incentive-change, price-reduction, duplicate, corrected, delayed, conflicted, missing, and stale input conditions.

The report measures false positives, misses, persistence, duplication, churn, materiality stability, conflict handling, expiration behavior, and reproducibility.

## Boundary

M13-008 does not add seller recommendations, autonomous contact, marketing, pricing, execution, seller-intent inference, protected-class or proxy use, unauthorized scraping, valuation-engine behavior, unsupported causation, hidden conflict resolution, or M12 circumvention.

Certified historical inputs are immutable. Corrections create explicit child replay cases and do not rewrite the original case.

## Acceptance

The boundary passes only if historical replay is deterministic; prior certified state remains immutable; duplicates, corrections, delayed/conflicted/missing/stale inputs are explicitly handled; drift and recovery conditions are surfaced; metric outputs are reproducible; inherited regressions remain green; and zero waivers/open defects remain.
