# M11-007 — Seller Intelligence Effectiveness & Outcome Learning Layer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Measure how certified M11-001→006 seller-intelligence signals, review events, communication, and recorded human decisions relate to observed downstream outcomes without rewriting history, inferring causation from correlation, or changing live strategy automatically.

## Frozen boundary

M11-007 may:

- register governed observed outcomes;
- link outcomes back to exact M11-001→006 artifact and decision fingerprints;
- measure whether predicted review conditions were followed by observable market-response changes;
- compare communication/review timing with later observed evidence;
- aggregate effectiveness by governed strategy state or monitoring condition;
- distinguish observation, association, and hypothesis;
- surface calibration candidates for later M11-008 review.

M11-007 does not:

- assert causal impact without governed evidence;
- rewrite prior strategy, scenario, timeline, communication, workspace, or human-decision records;
- infer seller motivation, acceptance, buyer behavior, or protected-class information;
- recommend or change a list price;
- predict a sale price or guaranteed proceeds;
- promote calibration changes automatically;
- approve, publish, schedule, queue, transmit, or execute external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M11-007 may be accepted only when observed outcomes are traceable to immutable prior artifacts, association is clearly separated from causation, missing/unknown outcomes remain explicit, hindsight does not mutate historical records, effectiveness outputs are deterministic, calibration candidates remain advisory only, public leakage is prevented, external action capability remains NONE, and inherited certification gates pass.
