# M11-007 — Seller Intelligence Effectiveness & Outcome Learning Layer

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Measure how certified M11-001→006 seller-intelligence signals, review events, communication, and recorded human decisions relate to observed downstream outcomes without rewriting history, inferring causation from correlation, or changing live strategy automatically.

## Accepted learning capabilities

M11-007 now:

- registers governed observed outcomes as immutable records;
- links outcomes to the exact certified M11-006 case;
- requires the exact certified M11-004 timeline referenced by that case;
- proves whether a review event actually occurred before an observed outcome;
- proves whether a recorded human decision actually occurred before an observed outcome;
- records those relationships as associations, not causal claims;
- keeps UNKNOWN outcomes explicit;
- produces advisory calibration candidates for M11-008;
- produces advisory outcome-coverage-gap candidates when observation quality is insufficient.

Implemented in:

- `registries/seller_intelligence/m11-007-effectiveness-learning-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/seller_intelligence/effectiveness.py`
- `tests/unit/seller_intelligence/test_effectiveness.py`
- `certification-evidence/m11-007/effectiveness-learning-acceptance-v1.0.json`

## Chronology and causation boundary

M11-007 does not infer “preceded by” from case membership alone.

A review event is associated as prior only when its certified M11-004 event timestamp is less than or equal to the observed outcome timestamp.

A human decision is associated as prior only when its recorded timestamp is less than or equal to the observed outcome timestamp.

All resulting language remains explicitly non-causal.

## Calibration boundary

Calibration candidates are:

- advisory only;
- `promotion_status: NOT_PROMOTED`;
- traceable to the observed outcome and association fingerprints;
- intended only for M11-008 controlled calibration review.

They do not mutate live strategy, historical records, or production rules.

## Output boundary

M11-007 remains:

- INTERNAL-only;
- `public_eligible: false`;
- `external_action_capability: NONE`.

It cannot recommend price, predict sale price or proceeds, infer seller/buyer behavior, promote changes automatically, publish, schedule, queue, transmit, or execute an external action.
