# M12-003 — Listing Action Verification, Reconciliation & Controlled Rollback Layer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Consume immutable M12-002 execution receipts and independently verify actual downstream state, reconcile receipt claims with observed external state, classify mismatches and outcome-unknown cases, and permit only controlled rollback paths that are separately authorized and fully traceable.

## Frozen boundary

M12-003 owns:

- post-execution verification requests;
- observed-state evidence registration;
- receipt-to-observed-state reconciliation;
- explicit VERIFIED / MISMATCH / OUTCOME_UNKNOWN / NOT_APPLICABLE states;
- reconciliation-case creation;
- corrective-action eligibility;
- rollback eligibility and exact rollback lineage;
- separate rollback approval and authority requirements;
- immutable verification and reconciliation receipts.

M12-003 does not:

- assume a successful receipt proves final state;
- silently resolve outcome-unknown;
- rewrite M12-002 receipts;
- infer seller approval;
- automatically roll back;
- bypass human approval or execution authority;
- determine price or terms;
- promote policy automatically.

## Acceptance boundary

M12-003 may be accepted only when every executed side effect can be independently verified or explicitly remain unknown, receipt/observed-state mismatches create governed reconciliation cases, outcome-unknown cannot be silently closed, rollback requires separate explicit approval and authority, rollback lineage points to the exact original proposal/authorization/execution/verification chain, all verification and rollback actions are idempotent, and inherited certification gates remain green.
