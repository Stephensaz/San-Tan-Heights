# M11-003 — Seller Scenario & Strategy Sensitivity Engine

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Take certified M11-002 property-level seller strategy and evaluate controlled what-if scenarios that help a human understand how the strategy context changes when governed assumptions or market conditions change.

## Frozen boundary

M11-003 may model scenario differences across governed inputs such as:

- buyer depth;
- competitive pressure;
- verified differentiation;
- new-construction pressure;
- pricing-response environment;
- week-over-week direction;
- review-trigger state.

M11-003 must preserve exact M11-002 lineage and clearly distinguish baseline evidence from hypothetical scenario inputs.

M11-003 does not:

- determine or recommend a list price;
- predict a sale price;
- estimate guaranteed net proceeds;
- predict seller acceptance or buyer behavior;
- infer seller motivation;
- infer protected-class information;
- convert hypothetical scenarios into asserted facts;
- expose seller strategy publicly by default;
- execute, schedule, queue, approve, or authorize external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M11-003 may be accepted only when scenario inputs are explicitly hypothetical, baseline versus scenario states are separately traceable, sensitivity outputs are deterministic, unsupported combinations fail closed, prohibited price/value/outcome fields cannot be generated, public eligibility remains false, external action capability remains NONE, and inherited certification gates pass.
