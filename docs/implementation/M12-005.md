# M12-005 — Listing Action Policy Calibration & Controlled Promotion Framework

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Take certified M12-004 advisory operational-improvement candidates and convert them into isolated candidate versions of listing-action and execution policy that can be replayed, compared, approved, promoted, rejected, and rolled back without mutating the accepted M12-001→004 baseline in place.

## Frozen boundary

M12-005 may:

- register certified M12-004 improvement candidates;
- bind each candidate to exact accepted policy/version fingerprints;
- create isolated candidate versions of action-generation rules, approval workflows, authority requirements, execution revalidation, adapter policies, verification rules, reconciliation behavior, and rollback controls;
- replay current versus candidate behavior side-effect-free over certified historical and synthetic fixtures;
- quantify safety, operational, verification, reconciliation, and rollback regressions;
- require explicit human promotion approval;
- preserve exact rollback to the previously accepted policy baseline;
- produce a controlled promotion package for later M12 certification.

M12-005 does not:

- mutate accepted policy in place;
- auto-promote a candidate;
- treat correlation as causal policy proof;
- weaken human approval, authority, idempotency, verification, reconciliation, rollback, or outcome-unknown controls;
- introduce system-determined price or terms;
- execute, publish, schedule, queue, or transmit external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M12-005 may be accepted only when candidate versions are isolated and deterministic, exact parent lineage is preserved, replay is side-effect-free, current-versus-candidate differences are explicit, any safety regression fails closed, promotion requires separate human approval, rollback lineage is complete, accepted policy is never mutated in place, and all inherited certification gates remain green.
