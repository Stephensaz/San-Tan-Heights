# M13-004 — Community Temporal History & Change Ledger

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Assemble certified M13-001 snapshots, M13-002 deltas, M13-003 refresh plans/results, corrections, conflicts, and version transitions into one immutable longitudinal memory for each property and the community.

M13-004 preserves two different clocks:

- **reality / valid time** — when a fact is effective in the modeled world;
- **knowledge / system time** — when the platform learned or recorded that fact.

Those clocks may not be collapsed.

## Hard prohibitions

Certified history may never be rewritten or deleted. Discovery time may not be substituted for valid time. Unknown dates remain unknown rather than being invented. Corrections append a new entry and link to the prior entry; conflicts and superseded evidence remain visible. UI/history queries are read-only. M13-004 does not create recommendations, listing actions, publication state, M12 execution mutations, or a competing identity/community truth system.

## Acceptance boundary
