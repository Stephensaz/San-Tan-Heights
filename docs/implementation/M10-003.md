# M10-003 — Community Onboarding Contract & Bootstrap Generator

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Turn the accepted M10-002 core/configuration separation into a governed onboarding contract and deterministic bootstrap generator for a new community without copying San Tan Heights-specific assumptions into reusable platform behavior.

## Accepted implementation

M10-003 adds:

- `contracts/evolution/STH-COMMUNITY-ONBOARDING-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/evolution/community_onboarding.py`
- `tests/unit/evolution/test_community_onboarding.py`
- `certification-evidence/m10-003/community-onboarding-acceptance-v1.0.json`
- contract-manifest registration for the locked onboarding contract.

The generator requires explicit community identity, jurisdiction, property-ID prefix, source authorities, parent-platform lineage, community-specific assumptions, and source-specific assumptions. It produces deterministic community-config and onboarding-manifest payloads with canonical SHA-256 fingerprints and no generated timestamps.

## Guardrails

- San Tan Heights cannot be silently re-onboarded as a new community.
- No San Tan Heights identity, county, MLS, or source default is injected.
- Missing/invalid identity, source, or lineage fields fail closed.
- Community-specific and source-specific assumptions must be explicit and nonempty.
- No production ingestion, intelligence method, or release certification occurs in M10-003.
- Accepted San Tan Heights baseline artifacts remain immutable.
