# M12-008 — Listing Execution Audit, Compliance Evidence & Operational Certification Layer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Assemble immutable audit and compliance evidence across M12-001→007, prove that every governed action, approval, authority, execution, verification, reconciliation, rollback, policy calibration, workspace state, incident hold, release, and recovery event is complete and reproducible, and produce an operational certification package for M12-009 without issuing the final Milestone 12 decision.

## Frozen boundary

M12-008 may:

- verify accepted evidence for M12-001→007;
- verify audit-chain completeness and exact lineage;
- verify immutable receipt and decision records;
- certify approval/authority separation;
- certify idempotency and outcome-unknown controls;
- certify verification/reconciliation/rollback controls;
- certify policy-calibration promotion boundaries;
- certify workspace no-action boundaries;
- certify incident containment/recovery evidence;
- detect missing, contradictory, stale, or unexplained audit gaps;
- produce an immutable operational-certification package for M12-009.

M12-008 does not:

- issue the final Milestone 12 PASS/GO decision;
- modify accepted M12-001→007 evidence;
- execute, publish, schedule, queue, or transmit actions;
- auto-promote policy;
- weaken approval, authority, verification, rollback, or incident controls.

## Acceptance boundary

M12-008 may be accepted only when every M12-001→007 acceptance criterion has executable evidence, all required lineage and immutable records reproduce, zero unexplained audit gaps remain, zero waivers and zero blocking defects remain, all safety invariants are covered by tests, and inherited certification gates remain green.
