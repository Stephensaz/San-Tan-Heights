# M10-008 — Community Factory Operating Model & Release Automation

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Convert the accepted M10-003→007 onboarding, progressive certification, portability, hardening, and cold-start capabilities into a governed repeatable community-factory operating model with deterministic release automation and explicit human decision gates.

## Frozen boundary

M10-008 owns factory job manifests, automated onboarding/build orchestration, profile-bundle assembly, stage execution sequencing, evidence collection, candidate packaging, release-request generation, deterministic run fingerprints, retry/resume rules, and human approval handoffs.

M10-008 does not automatically publish communities, does not bypass M10-004 stage gates, does not infer source/community assumptions, does not promote candidates without authority, does not weaken evidence requirements, and does not mutate accepted community baselines.

## Required implementation

- define a deterministic community-factory job manifest;
- consume M10-003 onboarding inputs and M10-006 governed profiles;
- invoke the M10-004 progressive pipeline through one controlled factory runner;
- collect stage evidence and candidate fingerprints automatically;
- distinguish machine-executable steps from required human decisions;
- implement fail-closed retry/resume behavior that cannot skip a failed stage;
- package an immutable release candidate without publishing it;
- generate a release-request artifact with lineage, evidence, unresolved exceptions, and approval requirements;
- prove both Rancho Vistoso and Daybreak can be represented by the same factory workflow without embedding either community as a core default;
- preserve accepted San Tan Heights, Rancho Vistoso, and Daybreak evidence unchanged.

## Acceptance boundary

M10-008 may be accepted only when the same factory runner can deterministically assemble and certify multiple community jobs, failed stages cannot be skipped during retry/resume, release packaging is non-publishing and authority-gated, community/source assumptions remain externalized, accepted evidence remains immutable, and inherited certification gates pass.
