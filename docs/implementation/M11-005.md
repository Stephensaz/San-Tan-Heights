# M11-005 — Seller Intelligence Communication Translation Layer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Translate certified M11-001→004 seller intelligence into clear human-facing communication without changing the underlying facts, strategy, scenario assumptions, monitoring decisions, qualifiers, eligibility, or lineage.

## Frozen boundary

M11-005 may produce governed communication projections for authorized internal, agent, and seller use, including:

- concise explanation of current buyer alternatives and competitive pressure;
- plain-language strategy summaries;
- scenario explanations that remain explicitly hypothetical;
- timeline/change summaries;
- human-review-required monitoring explanations;
- preserved limitations, evidence gaps, freshness, qualifiers, and lineage references.

M11-005 must keep factual statements, hypothetical assumptions, and monitoring guidance distinguishable.

M11-005 does not:

- invent or strengthen facts;
- remove material qualifiers or limitations;
- determine or recommend a list price;
- predict a sale price or guaranteed proceeds;
- guarantee outcomes;
- infer seller motivation, acceptance, buyer behavior, or protected-class information;
- expose private seller strategy publicly by default;
- convert communication into approval, publication, scheduling, or any external action.

## Acceptance boundary

M11-005 may be accepted only when communication output is deterministic, every statement traces to eligible certified intelligence or clearly labeled hypothetical context, qualifiers/limitations/freshness are preserved, prohibited price/value/outcome language cannot be generated, tier isolation is enforced, public strategy leakage is prevented, external action capability remains NONE, and inherited certification gates pass.
