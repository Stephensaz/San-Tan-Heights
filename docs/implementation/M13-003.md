# M13-003 — Temporal Impact Propagation & Selective Intelligence Refresh

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Consume a certified M13-002 community delta, resolve its governed dependency impact against the current certified derived-intelligence state, refresh only invalidated artifacts through authoritative owning engines, preserve unaffected certified state exactly, and prove why every artifact was refreshed or preserved.

M13-003 answers **what must be refreshed because of a certified change, and what must remain untouched**.

## Implementation boundary

M13-003 owns:

- deterministic dependency matching from certified M13-002 changes;
- artifact-level REFRESH_REQUIRED versus NO_IMPACT decisions;
- exact source-change lineage for every refresh decision;
- explicit no-impact reason codes for unaffected artifacts;
- exact refresh-set construction;
- authoritative replacement validation;
- after-snapshot binding for replacement artifacts;
- preservation of unaffected certified records without regeneration;
- deterministic plan/result replay.

M13-003 does not implement pricing, buyer-depth, comparable, scarcity, competition, scenario, or Seller Intelligence formulas. Recalculated replacements must come from their authoritative owning engines.

## Hard prohibitions

M13-003 does not:

- consume uncertified or nonreproducible M13-002 deltas;
- mutate M13-001 snapshots or M13-002 deltas;
- blanket-refresh every derived artifact;
- refresh an artifact without a governed dependency match;
- locally recalculate domain intelligence;
- accept missing or extra refresh replacements;
- accept a replacement bound to the wrong after-snapshot;
- cross-contaminate property-scoped impacts;
- change unaffected certified artifacts;
- create seller recommendations;
- create listing actions;
- publish externally;
- mutate M12 execution state;
- propagate protected fields.

## M13-004 boundary

M13-003 performs deterministic refresh orchestration only. Historical learning, outcome inference, calibration, pattern promotion, or policy learning remain outside this ticket.

## Acceptance boundary

M13-003 may be accepted only after deterministic impact/no-impact decisions, selective refresh, exact unaffected-state preservation, authoritative replacement lineage, tamper detection, property isolation, and inherited M13/M12 regression gates are green with zero waivers and zero open defects.
