# M9-010 — Controlled Publication Cohorts

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Activate publication only through controlled, governed cohorts after accepted M9-009 full-corpus QA, while preserving audience-tier eligibility, current-publication pointer semantics, immutable history, rollback safety, and zero publication of quarantined or stale/ineligible content.

## Frozen inputs

- M9-001 through M9-009 accepted outputs.
- accepted M7 production certification / progressive rollout controls.
- accepted M8 Agent, Seller, and Public presentation/publication contracts.
- locked M9 Production Population & Community Activation contract.

## Hard rules

- staged cohorts only;
- no publication outside governed cohort membership;
- Agent/Seller/Public tier eligibility remains exact;
- quarantined or ineligible content cannot publish;
- stale current-competition findings remain suppressed;
- current publication is pointer-based;
- accepted historical artifacts remain immutable;
- invalidated history never becomes automatic fallback;
- every publication must retain exact report, Passport, policy, cohort, and source lineage;
- rollback must preserve history and restore only a previously governed safe publication state;
- no new intelligence or presentation semantics.

## Acceptance boundary

M9-010 is accepted only when controlled cohort publication proves exact membership, publication eligibility, tier isolation, pointer integrity, complete lineage, zero quarantined/stale leakage, deterministic cohort accounting, and governed rollback behavior without activating unrestricted fleet publication.


## Implementation

M9-010 binds the accepted M9 activation layer to the already-certified M7 publication controls rather than creating a parallel rollout system.

Implemented artifacts:

- `registries/activation/m9-010-controlled-publication-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/activation/controlled_publication.py`
- `tests/unit/activation/test_controlled_publication.py`

The runtime enforces:

- frozen rollout order: `COHORT_25 → COHORT_100 → COHORT_500 → REMAINING_FLEET`;
- deterministic property selection and exclusion of prior-cohort membership;
- exact Agent/Seller/Public tier binding;
- fail-closed rejection of missing, ineligible, quarantined, or stale-current report variants;
- manual-by-default publication inherited from M7;
- pointer-based publication rather than historical artifact mutation;
- compare-and-swap pointer updates to prevent stale publication batches;
- rollback to the exact captured pointer baseline;
- no automatic fallback to invalidated history;
- M9-009 ACCEPTED as an explicit prerequisite;
- locked M7 progressive-rollout and manual-publication policies as upstream controls.

M9-010 remains `IN_PROGRESS` until its live CI and inherited M8 presentation certification gates pass.
