# M9-008 — Exception Quarantine & Repair Workflow

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Establish the production exception-quarantine and repair workflow for all M9 population/materialization layers so unresolved, invalid, stale, or failed records are isolated without contaminating accepted property/report outputs.

## Frozen inputs

- M9-001 through M9-007 accepted outputs.
- existing governed exception classes from the M9 activation contract.
- accepted upstream defect ownership and fail-closed repair rules.

## Hard rules

- every exception must have an explicit class, owning layer, reason, severity, and repair state;
- exceptions may not silently disappear from accounting, including after closure;
- quarantined records cannot be published or substituted with inferred values;
- upstream-owned defects must be repaired in the owning layer, then downstream layers replayed;
- historical accepted artifacts remain immutable;
- stale/expired/unavailable conditions are not automatically defects and do not become publishable merely because they are non-defects;
- the known frontage exception `STH-516018120` remains governed until its upstream evidence is legitimately repaired;
- no waiver may convert an unresolved exception into publishable content;
- repair replay must preserve unaffected fingerprints and lineage.

## Implementation

M9-008 adds a frozen exception policy and deterministic runtime for exception classification, ownership, quarantine, repair-state accounting, and replay isolation.

### Implemented artifacts

- `registries/activation/m9-008-exception-quarantine-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/activation/exception_quarantine.py`
- `tests/unit/activation/test_exception_quarantine.py`

### Governed behavior

The runtime now enforces:

- explicit exception class, owning layer, reason, severity, repair state, and scope;
- fail-closed rejection of unknown classes, invalid states, or ownership mismatch;
- permanent exception accounting: a prior exception cannot silently disappear, even after closure;
- supersession lineage for corrected/repaired exception records;
- quarantine/publication separation;
- no inferred substitution for missing or quarantined evidence;
- waiver attempts cannot create publishability;
- stale/unavailable current-context states remain non-defect governed conditions while affected content remains non-publishable;
- the `STH-516018120` unresolved frontage condition remains explicitly governed under M9-003 ownership;
- repair replay may change only the declared downstream dependency chain;
- unaffected fingerprints must remain stable.

### Known upstream state preserved

M9-008 does not reinterpret previously accepted states:

- `STH-516018120` remains the single partial frontage/spatial case from M9-003.
- M9-004 unresolved/non-promoted historical records remain non-promoted and are not assigned by inference.
- M9-005 unavailable/stale/expired conditions remain explicit conditions rather than automatically becoming defects.
- M9-006/M9-007 tier suppression and report materialization semantics remain unchanged.
