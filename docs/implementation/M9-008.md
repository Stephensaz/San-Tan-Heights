# M9-008 — Exception Quarantine & Repair Workflow

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Establish the production exception-quarantine and repair workflow for all M9 population/materialization layers so unresolved, invalid, stale, or failed records are isolated without contaminating accepted property/report outputs.

## Frozen inputs

- M9-001 through M9-007 accepted outputs.
- existing governed exception classes from the M9 activation contract.
- accepted upstream defect ownership and fail-closed repair rules.

## Hard rules

- every exception must have an explicit class, owning layer, reason, severity, and repair state;
- exceptions may not silently disappear from accounting;
- quarantined records cannot be published or substituted with inferred values;
- upstream-owned defects must be repaired in the owning layer, then downstream layers replayed;
- historical accepted artifacts remain immutable;
- stale/expired/unavailable conditions are not automatically defects;
- the known frontage exception `STH-516018120` remains governed until its upstream evidence is legitimately repaired;
- no waiver may convert an unresolved exception into publishable content;
- repair replay must preserve unaffected fingerprints and lineage.

## Implementation

The M9-008 implementation adds:

- `registries/activation/m9-008-exception-quarantine-v1.0.yaml` — frozen exception classes, defect ownership, quarantine rules, the governed frontage exception, and non-defect stale/unavailable classifications.
- `src/activation/exception_quarantine.py` — deterministic exception evaluation, ledger accounting, silent-disappearance protection, immutable fingerprints, and repair-replay isolation checks.
- `tests/unit/activation/test_exception_quarantine.py` — adversarial coverage for waiver attempts, wrong ownership, stale-state classification, known-exception accounting, silent disappearance, supersession, and unaffected-fingerprint preservation.
