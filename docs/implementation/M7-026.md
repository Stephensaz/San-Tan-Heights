# M7-026 — Formal FULL_APPROVAL

Accepted in 0.1.47.

FULL_APPROVAL is issued only under the locked policy when the final evidence bundle is PASS, the candidate fingerprint matches, go-live stop conditions are CLEAR, no matching revocation exists, and COHORT_25, COHORT_100, COHORT_500, and REMAINING_FLEET certifications are all PASS. The approval is immutable historical evidence.
