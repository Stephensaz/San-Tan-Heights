# M8-011 — Lot / Context Diagram Renderer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Render the locked diagram grammar into deterministic schematic lot/context SVG without introducing geographic precision or property-value interpretation.

## Rendering behavior

- Subject homesite is visually centered and identified without implying quality.
- `FRONTS`, `BACKS_TO`, and `SIDES_TO` use relative front/rear/side zones.
- Other governed relationships use neutral context zones.
- The renderer consumes the locked design-token registry for presentation styling.
- Governed source-finding identifiers remain in the render model for traceability but are not exposed in the SVG.
- Every SVG states that it is a schematic relationship diagram, not a map, and not to scale.
- Compass directions, coordinates, distances, prices, premiums, and desirability claims are not created.
