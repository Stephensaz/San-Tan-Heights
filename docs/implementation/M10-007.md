# M10-007 — Third-Community Cold-Start Validation & Repeatability

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Prove that the M10-001→006 portability system can onboard and progress a third materially distinct community from a cold start without relying on prior-pilot-specific configuration or hidden community assumptions.

## Accepted third community

The third-community cold-start validation uses **Daybreak in South Jordan, Salt Lake County, Utah**.

Material differences from the first two communities include:

- different state: Utah;
- different county: Salt Lake County;
- different municipality: South Jordan;
- different listing-service boundary: UtahRealEstate.com / Wasatch Front Regional MLS;
- different parcel authority: Salt Lake County Assessor;
- different recorder authority: Salt Lake County Recorder;
- different community-planning taxonomy and public GIS environment.

The real community identity is used only as the portability boundary. The test property corpus remains synthetic. No proprietary MLS records and no homeowner personal data are used.

## Clean-room proof

M10-007:

- creates a fresh M10-003 onboarding bootstrap;
- creates four Daybreak-specific governed profiles through the M10-006 interfaces;
- explicitly rejects any dependency on prior pilot profile paths or identifiers;
- executes the full eight-stage M10-004 progressive pipeline;
- requires source provenance for community, listing, parcel, and recorder boundaries;
- preserves the synthetic-data boundary;
- rejects manual remediation actions;
- rejects implied proprietary MLS access.

## Measured repeatability

The Daybreak cold start contains **11 observable engineering work items**:

- CORE_REUSABLE: 4
- COMMUNITY_CONFIGURABLE: 3
- COMMUNITY_SPECIFIC: 1
- SOURCE_SPECIFIC: 3

Actions:

- reused unchanged: 4/11;
- configured through governed interfaces: 7/11;
- manual remediation: **0/11**.

Comparison:

- Rancho Vistoso before M10-006 hardening: **4/11 manual remediation**;
- Rancho Vistoso after M10-006 hardening: **0/11**;
- Daybreak third-community cold start: **0/11**.

This demonstrates that the reduction in manual remediation is repeatable on a materially different third community rather than being a Rancho Vistoso-only optimization.
