# M10-007 — Third-Community Cold-Start Validation & Repeatability

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Prove that the M10-001→006 portability system can onboard and progress a third materially distinct community from a cold start without relying on Rancho Vistoso-specific remediation history or hidden San Tan Heights assumptions.

## Frozen boundary

M10-007 owns third-community selection, clean-room onboarding inputs, cold-start bootstrap generation, governed profile preparation, execution through the M10-004 progressive pipeline, repeatability measurement, and comparison against the M10-005/M10-006 portability experience.

M10-007 does not reuse Rancho Vistoso-specific configuration as a shortcut, does not publish the third community, does not declare the community factory production-ready, does not bypass source/community classification, and does not mutate accepted San Tan Heights or Rancho Vistoso evidence.

## Required proof

- select a third real community materially distinct from both San Tan Heights and Rancho Vistoso;
- begin from only the accepted platform contracts and documented public/source boundaries;
- generate a clean M10-003 onboarding bootstrap;
- use M10-006 governed profile interfaces where applicable;
- execute the complete M10-004 pipeline;
- measure configuration versus manual remediation from a cold start;
- prove no Rancho Vistoso-specific artifacts were required;
- prove no San Tan Heights defaults leaked;
- produce deterministic repeatability fingerprints;
- compare third-community effort with the pre-hardening and post-hardening Rancho Vistoso results.

## Acceptance boundary

M10-007 may be accepted only when the third community completes the full cold-start pipeline deterministically, no prior-pilot-specific configuration is required, all source/community differences remain explicit, manual remediation does not depend on hidden defaults, accepted evidence remains immutable, and inherited certification gates pass.
