# M11-002 — Property-Level Seller Strategy Engine

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Consume certified M11-001 seller-opportunity findings and synthesize a property-level seller strategy view without determining value, recommending a list price, predicting a sale price, guaranteeing an outcome, or performing external actions.

## Frozen boundary

M11-002 may synthesize:

- current buyer alternatives;
- competitive pressure;
- buyer depth;
- verified property differentiation;
- new-construction pressure;
- pricing-response environment;
- week-over-week direction;
- Day-7 / Day-10 / Day-14 review triggers.

M11-002 must preserve exact M11-001 lineage and evidence eligibility.

M11-002 does not:

- determine or recommend a list price;
- predict a sale price;
- guarantee seller outcomes;
- infer seller motivation or behavior;
- infer protected-class information;
- expose seller strategy publicly by default;
- execute, schedule, queue, or authorize external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M11-002 may be accepted only when strategy synthesis is deterministic, every strategy element is traceable to certified eligible findings, missing or stale evidence remains explicit, review triggers are evidence-based rather than prescriptive, prohibited price/value/outcome fields cannot be generated, public eligibility remains false, external action capability remains NONE, and inherited certification gates pass.
