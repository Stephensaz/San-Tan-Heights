# M11-002 — Property-Level Seller Strategy Engine

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Consume certified M11-001 seller-opportunity findings and synthesize a property-level seller strategy view without determining value, recommending a list price, predicting a sale price, guaranteeing an outcome, or performing external actions.

## Accepted synthesis

M11-002 combines:

- current buyer alternatives;
- competitive pressure;
- buyer depth;
- verified property differentiation;
- new-construction pressure;
- pricing-response environment;
- week-over-week direction;
- Day-7 / Day-10 / Day-14 review triggers.

Implemented in:

- `registries/seller_intelligence/m11-002-property-seller-strategy-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/seller_intelligence/strategy.py`
- `tests/unit/seller_intelligence/test_strategy.py`
- `certification-evidence/m11-002/property-seller-strategy-acceptance-v1.0.json`

## Evidence handling

Every governed strategy dimension must be traceable to certified eligible evidence.

If evidence is unavailable:

- no inferred value is substituted;
- the dimension is marked `MISSING`.

If evidence exists but is stale, QA-failed, or not SELLER-eligible:

- it is not promoted;
- the dimension is marked `SUPPRESSED`;
- the reason remains visible through the strategy limitation set.

## Review checkpoints

Day-7, Day-10, and Day-14 outputs are review checkpoints only.

They:

- expose governed reasons for review;
- do not prescribe a price change;
- do not recommend a list price;
- do not predict a sale price;
- do not authorize an external action.

## Output boundary

The strategy remains:

- SELLER-only;
- `public_eligible: false`;
- `external_action_capability: NONE`.

Prohibited fields for price recommendations, sale-price predictions, valuation, seller motivation, guaranteed outcomes, and automatic action do not exist in the strategy DTO.
