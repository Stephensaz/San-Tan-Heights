# M11-006 — Seller Intelligence Workspace & Case Management

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Assemble certified M11-001→005 seller intelligence, monitoring events, communication projections, evidence limitations, review state, and human decisions into one governed property-level workspace without creating a new intelligence, valuation, pricing, publication, or execution engine.

## Accepted workspace capabilities

M11-006 now:

- creates one governed seller-intelligence case per property;
- registers current certified M11-001→005 artifacts;
- keeps superseded strategy snapshots visible;
- converts monitoring events into explicit review items;
- tracks review status as PENDING, ACKNOWLEDGED, RESOLVED, or SUPERSEDED;
- records human decisions separately from machine intelligence;
- requires an explicit human decision to acknowledge or resolve a review item;
- preserves limitations and evidence gaps;
- exposes only the next safe human-review step.

Implemented in:

- `registries/seller_intelligence/m11-006-seller-workspace-v1.0.yaml`
- `src/seller_intelligence/workspace.py`
- `tests/unit/seller_intelligence/test_workspace.py`
- `certification-evidence/m11-006/seller-workspace-acceptance-v1.0.json`

## Machine versus human boundary

Machine-generated intelligence remains immutable input.

Human decisions are separately recorded with:

- decision ID;
- review-item reference;
- decision type;
- actor ID;
- decision timestamp;
- rationale;
- decision fingerprint.

Human decisions do not mutate upstream M11 artifacts.

## Review-state boundary

A review item cannot silently disappear.

ACKNOWLEDGED and RESOLVED states require explicit human decision records. Earlier unresolved items from superseded snapshots remain visible as SUPERSEDED rather than being deleted.

## Output boundary

The workspace remains:

- INTERNAL-only;
- `public_eligible: false`;
- `external_action_capability: NONE`.

It cannot recommend price, alter strategy automatically, approve, publish, schedule, queue, transmit, or execute an external action.
