# M12-007 — Listing Execution Operations Monitoring, Incident Containment & Recovery Layer

Status: ACCEPTED

## Purpose

Monitor certified M12 execution operations for material failures, adapter degradation, repeated outcome-unknown events, reconciliation backlog, verification failures, authority anomalies, and rollback incidents; place affected work into explicit operational holds; support governed containment and recovery; and preserve complete incident lineage without bypassing human approval or execution authority.

## Accepted incident model

M12-007 now detects governed incident conditions including:

- adapter degraded;
- adapter unavailable;
- outcome unknown;
- open reconciliation;
- authority anomaly;
- repeated outcome unknown across multiple items.

Each incident preserves exact workspace/evidence lineage and an explicit blast radius.

## Containment

Incidents may create scoped holds at:

- action level;
- listing level;
- adapter level.

The incident-guarded execution path checks active holds before any adapter call.

Held work therefore cannot execute through the governed path.

## Recovery boundary

A hold cannot be silently cleared.

Recovery requires:

- explicit human `RELEASE` decision;
- fresh prerequisite revalidation;
- fresh authority revalidation;
- exact hold/incident/recovery lineage.

For `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` or repeated unknown incidents, independent reconciliation must be complete before recovery may close.

## Prohibited behavior

M12-007 does not:

- automatically retry unknown work;
- infer seller approval;
- bypass approval or authority;
- execute corrective work autonomously;
- determine price or terms;
- mutate accepted policy;
- publish, schedule, queue, or transmit external actions.
