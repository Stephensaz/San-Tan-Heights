# M12-007 — Listing Execution Operations Monitoring, Incident Containment & Recovery Layer

Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Monitor certified M12 execution operations for material failures, adapter degradation, repeated outcome-unknown events, reconciliation backlog, verification failures, authority anomalies, and rollback incidents; place affected work into explicit operational holds; support governed containment and recovery; and preserve complete incident lineage without bypassing human approval or execution authority.

## Frozen boundary

M12-007 may:

- register immutable operational incidents;
- detect governed incident conditions from certified M12-002→006 artifacts;
- place actions/listings/adapters into explicit execution holds;
- surface containment scope and blast radius;
- track investigation, mitigation, recovery, and closure state;
- require human release from hold;
- require fresh authority and prerequisite revalidation after recovery;
- preserve exact incident/evidence/action/receipt lineage;
- produce incident and recovery evidence for M12-008.

M12-007 does not:

- silently clear a hold;
- automatically retry outcome-unknown work;
- infer seller approval;
- bypass approval or authority;
- execute corrective work autonomously;
- determine price or terms;
- mutate accepted policy;
- publish, schedule, queue, or transmit external actions.

## Acceptance boundary

M12-007 may be accepted only when incident conditions are deterministic and traceable, containment fails closed, held work cannot execute through the governed path, release requires explicit human decision and fresh revalidation, outcome-unknown remains unresolved until independently reconciled, incident history is immutable, recovery evidence is complete, and inherited certification gates remain green.
