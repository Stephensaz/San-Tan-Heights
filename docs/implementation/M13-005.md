## Core boundary

Patterns are historical descriptive associations. They remain bound to explicit populations, regimes, time windows, confounder manifests, sample counts, validation sets, replication sets, temporal-ledger lineage, limitations, and exact promotion reasons.

Discovery populations cannot be silently reused as validation or replication populations. Null, negative, and failed-replication results remain first-class governed records.

Production eligibility is fail-closed and requires independent validation plus replication under the frozen registry thresholds.

## Hard prohibitions

M13-005 does not predict future outcomes, create AVMs, recommend list prices or actions, rank Sellers/listings/scenarios, select winners, infer causal effects without governed causal evidence, suppress unfavorable results, cherry-pick populations after observing results, mutate M13-004 history, publish externally, or mutate M12 execution state.

M13-005L must replay A→K from a clean environment and prove reproducible populations/regimes, discovery/validation/replication separation, exact temporal lineage, negative-result preservation, promotion safeguards, integration with accepted M13-003/M13-004, zero critical/blocking defects, zero waivers, and a machine-derived PASS/GO or FAIL/NO-GO result.
