# M7-009 — Manual Audit Sample Builder

Status: ACCEPTED

Implemented a locked manual-audit policy and deterministic sample builder over the frozen pilot population.

Key controls:
- sample selection is reproducible from production-certification ID, shadow-cycle ID, policy version, and property ID;
- properties with failed shadow targets are prioritized before deterministic hash-ranked fill;
- the sample is capped by the locked policy and never includes a property outside the frozen pilot population;
- the sample fingerprint represents the exact selected property set and source cycle;
- the builder does not compute shadow acceptance or go-live approval.
