Status: IN_PROGRESS

## Purpose

Execute the final Milestone 9 production-activation certification against the exact accepted M9-001 through M9-011 implementation and evidence set, prove all frozen activation acceptance criteria, and issue the formal Milestone 9 PASS / GO or FAIL / NO-GO decision.

## Frozen inputs

- M9-001 through M9-011 accepted outputs and evidence.
- locked M9 Production Population & Community Activation contract.
- accepted M1–M8 platform, production-certification, publication, and presentation controls.

## Hard rules

- evaluate the exact frozen production-activation candidate;
- every corpus member accounted for;
- zero unclassified exceptions;
- zero unauthorized publications;
- zero audience-tier leaks;
- complete required lineage for publishable records;
- unchanged replay fingerprints stable;
- governed changed records regenerate only through valid dependencies;
- refresh replay passes;
- rollback replay passes;
- all required certification evidence present and immutable;
- open defects must be zero;
- waivers must be zero;
- conditional GO is prohibited;
- M9-012 may certify or reject but may not redesign upstream architecture.

## Acceptance boundary

The only formal outcomes are `PASS / GO` or `FAIL / NO-GO`. A PASS / GO certifies the exact M9 production-activation candidate and closes Milestone 9; it does not authorize new architecture outside the already-frozen milestone sequence.
